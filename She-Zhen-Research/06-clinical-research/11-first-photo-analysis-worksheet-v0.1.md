# She-Zhen — First Photo Analysis Worksheet v0.1

Status: pilot observation template; not a diagnostic instrument.

## Purpose and limits

This worksheet records visible image features and image quality. It does not diagnose disease, identify internal-organ conditions, infer a TCM pattern, or recommend treatment. Traditional interpretations, if later discussed, must be recorded in a separate field with their source and evidence level.

## H. Prohibited conclusions in this pilot

Do not state from the photograph alone:
- a disease diagnosis;
- an internal-organ diagnosis;
- a TCM syndrome/pattern diagnosis;
- fertility status, pregnancy status, or any other sensitive health status;
- a treatment recommendation or prognosis.

Any later clinical research requires an appropriate protocol, qualified reviewers, consent, and ethics review as applicable.
